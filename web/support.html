WishTogether Support

WishTogether is run by one person. Email us and we will help.

Contact

Email christopherkelly92@gmail.com. Please include the phone number or email address you sign in with, what you were trying to do, and roughly when it happened. That is usually enough for us to find the problem.

Common questions

  • I did not get my verification code. Check the number, wait a minute, then request a new code. If it still does not arrive, email us.
  • I cannot see a shared wishlist. Ask the group's owner for a fresh invite link — access comes from being a member of the group the list is shared with.
  • I want to delete my account. Open the app, go to Profile, and choose Delete account. That removes your account and the lists you own. Items you claimed in someone else's list stay claimed, so the same gift is not bought twice.
  • Something looks wrong. Email us with the details above; we read every message.
